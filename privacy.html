Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Daily Faith & Planner (created by Planners by Lyubka). We deeply value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Daily Faith & Planner does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data on external servers.

  • Local Device Storage: All user data, including daily tasks, journal entries, personal notes, chosen scripture settings, and schedule times, are saved strictly on your local device via local storage (localStorage).
  • Cover Photos: Any image selected for the notebook cover remains solely on your device. We do not transmit or view your personal media.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Notifications: The app requests notification permissions solely to provide scheduled daily scripture verses and timely task reminders. All alarms are triggered locally on your device.
  • Storage / Media: The app accesses media solely when you actively select a photo to customize your personal notebook cover.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The app does not contain analytics tracking, advertising identifiers, third-party trackers, or commercial monetization algorithms. It operates as a personal offline devotional companion.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security of Your Data

Because your entries and settings reside directly on your smartphone, your information remains as secure as your personal device. Uninstalling the application or clearing your app storage will delete locally saved planner entries.
